## Choose the surface mode

- For an operational product surface such as a dashboard, admin tool, editor, or repeated workflow, read [product-ui-direction.md](../../references/product-ui-direction.md). Prioritize the real workspace, incumbent tokens and components, scanning, stable controls, and task completion over marketing composition.
- For a landing page, brand site, visual prototype, demo, or image-led experience, read [landing-page-composition.md](../../references/landing-page-composition.md). Use its viewport budget, full-bleed composition, imagery, copy, and restrained-motion guidance.
- Do not load both by default. Load both only when the request explicitly spans a marketing surface and its product interface.
