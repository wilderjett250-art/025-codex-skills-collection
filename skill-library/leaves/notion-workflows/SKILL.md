# Notion Workflows

Select one lane, read its canonical guide completely, and resolve its resources from the canonical directory:

- Capture decisions, chats, notes, how-tos, or FAQs: `canonical/notion-knowledge-capture/SKILL.md`
- Prepare agendas, pre-reads, and attendee-specific meeting material: `canonical/notion-meeting-intelligence/SKILL.md`
- Research across Notion sources and produce cited documentation: `canonical/notion-research-documentation/SKILL.md`
- Turn Notion PRDs or feature specs into plans and tasks: `canonical/notion-spec-to-implementation/SKILL.md`

If the Notion connector is unavailable or unauthenticated, state that prerequisite instead of pretending the workspace was read or changed.
